# Raw data

Place locally collected source files here. This directory is git-ignored to
reduce the risk of publishing personal or sensitive complaint data.

Before using a source, document its publisher, original URL, license or survey
consent basis, acquisition date, and any transformations in
`source_manifest.template.csv`. Remove direct identifiers and confirm you have
permission to use the data. Keep a separate protected original when required by
the applicable data-handling rules.
